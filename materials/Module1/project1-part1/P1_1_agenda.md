### Project 1 Part 1: Project launch and group outline

**Lecture anchor:** Lecture 9

## Lesson Plan (50 minutes)

- 5 min Getting started: form project groups, assign a shared Google Doc, and review the Project 1 deliverables
- 5 min Section 1: choose a working project title and record group members
- 8 min Sections 2–3: describe the topic, motivation, data source, observational unit, and important variables
- 8 min Section 4: write a focused research question and revise any causal wording
- 8 min Section 5: identify variables of interest and decide what the numerical and categorical analyses will examine
- 8 min Sections 6–7: plan summary statistics, visualizations, frequency/contingency tables, and interpretations
- 5 min Sections 8–9: identify data-quality concerns and list the visual evidence the group expects to produce
- 3 min Section 10 and closing: assign roles, set the next meeting, and complete the submission checklist

Groups should work in the shared document throughout the session. Submit one completed outline per group.

## Before class

- Post or import `P1_1_activity.docx` as the Google-Docs-ready Project 1 outline; retain `P1_1_activity.md` as the source template
- Have the Project 1 rubric and example presentation materials available
- Remind students to choose a dataset they can access and cite
- Prepare groups and confirm that every student has access to the shared document

## Notes

- Push groups toward descriptive, comparative, or association questions; causal questions should be revised before submission.
- The outline must name the observational unit, data source, variables of interest, planned numerical EDA, planned categorical EDA, and a data-quality check.
- Students do not need to complete the analyses today. The goal is a defensible plan that can guide later work.
- Check that roles are assigned to concrete tasks and that the next meeting or check-in time is recorded.
- After class: collect one completed Google Doc outline per group.
