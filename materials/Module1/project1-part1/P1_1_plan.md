### Project1-1: Project 1 launch

**Lecture anchor:** Lecture 9
**Purpose:** Launch Project 1 and guide each group to a focused, feasible research question and a first exploratory-data-analysis plan.

**In-class activity:** Start from `P1_1_activity.docx` in Google Docs, then complete it as one shared document per group. `P1_1_activity.md` remains the editable source template.

**Activities**
- Review the project prompt, rubric, data-access expectations, and timeline
- Choose a topic and data source, then describe the observational unit and important variables
- Write a non-causal research question and identify numerical and categorical variables of interest
- Plan visualizations, summary tables, data-quality checks, roles, and the next group meeting

**Post-class activity**
- Submit one completed Google Doc outline per group

**Deliverables**
- Completed Project 1 Week 1 Google Doc outline
- Project title, research question, cited data source, EDA plan, data-quality check, and group roles
