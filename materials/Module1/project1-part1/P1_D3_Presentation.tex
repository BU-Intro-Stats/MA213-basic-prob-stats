\begin{center}
\textbf{\Large Deliverable 3 \\ Recorded Presentation}
\end{center}

For your final deliverable, you will submit:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Revised slides for your video presentation (based on the feedback you received on Deliverable 2)
    \item Revised R code used to generate the visualizations and analyses (based on the feedback you received on Deliverable 2)
    \item A pre-recorded $\sim$5 min video presentation of your analyses, following the suggested outline in \texttt{P1\_D3\_Presentation.pdf}.
\end{enumerate}

You will post the video presentation to the Blackboard course page for your peers to watch. 
Then, you will ask at least three questions about the other groups' presentations, and you 
will also answer at least three questions about your own presentation.

Suggested outline for your recorded presentation:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Introduction
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Introduce the dataset and its source
        \item Tell the story of your reasoning and possible hypothesis
        \item Define your variables of interest
    \end{enumerate}
    \item Data Analysis
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Numerical Exploratory Data Analysis Part
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item Summary statistics
            \item The relationship between two numerical variables
            \item Distribution of variable(s) of interest
            \item Discuss shape, central tendency, spreadity and outliers.
        \end{enumerate}
        \item Categorical Exploratory Data Analysis Part
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item Summarize categorical variables
            \item Contingency Table
            \item Visualizations: bar plots, pie charts, etc.
        \end{enumerate}
    \end{enumerate}
    \item Conclusion
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item Summarize key insights
            \item Suggest future analysis or potential applications
        \end{enumerate}
\end{itemize}
