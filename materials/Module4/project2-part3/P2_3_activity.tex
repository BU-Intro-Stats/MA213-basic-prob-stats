\subsection*{Goal for Today}
Get feedback on your Deliverable 2 results and R code, plan who writes each part of the Deliverable 3 report, and practice explaining your whole project out loud to another group. By the end of lab, you should know exactly what to fix, and every part of the report should have an owner.

\subsection*{Before You Start}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item Open your group's P2\_D2\_Results doc, your R script, your data file, and a copy of the \textbf{P2\_D3\_Report} template (make a copy and share it with your group if you haven't yet).
    \item Everyone fills out their own sheet. Check off each step as your group finishes it, and hand in your sheet at the end of lab.
    \item The first half of lab will be for steps 1--2, and the second half will be for steps 3--4.
\end{itemize}
