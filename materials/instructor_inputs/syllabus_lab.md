# Syllabus Lab

## Skills Labs

Lab sections provide opportunities to work in groups, solve problems in probability and data analysis, and use R with RStudio. 

Skills labs are evaluated after submission of the in-lab worksheet. Students may revise and resubmit a lab deliverable before the next lab session. Lab topics and deliverables are generated from `lab_summary.md` and `Lab_schedules.md`.

## Lab Projects

Students work in groups to complete two projects:

- Project 1: video presentation
- Project 2: written report

Each project is evaluated with the ESRN rubric:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| E | Excellent or Exemplary |
| S | Satisfactory |
| R | Revisions Needed |
| N | Not Assessable |

A grade of **S** receives full credit for the project while grades of **E** may be considered when deciding between final letter grades. Projects receiving an **R** or **N** must be revised and resubmitted once. Any project that do not achieve an **S** or **E** by the end of the course will result in a reduction to your final course grade (see above).

### Peer Evaluation

During the project deliverables, each group member will submit a peer assessment form after each deliverable. The peer evaluation rubric includes the following categories: **Excellent**, **Satisfactory** and **Unsatisfactory**. If you consistently receive **unsatisfactory** ratings from the peer evaluation form, the lab instructor will reach out to you. Repeated **unsatisfactory** ratings in peer evaluation may result in a reduction of your final project grade.

## Tutorials

Interactive tutorials developed to prepare for
the skills labs. Each tutorial includes guided explanations, R examples, and exercises.
Each tutorial is available as an interactive lesson and is intended to be completed before the associated lab.
