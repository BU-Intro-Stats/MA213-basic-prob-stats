\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Lesson Plan}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item 6 min Lecture: A Continuous Parameter --- drug trial motivation, discrete/continuous RV reminder, Bayes' rule for RVs, evidence as normalizing constant
        \item 5 min Lecture: Likelihood and Prior on a Grid --- binomial likelihood, encoding prior belief, computing a discrete posterior
        \item 6 min Lecture: From Discrete to Continuous --- likelihood as a continuous curve, continuous prior, the Beta distribution and its properties
        \item 4 min Lecture: The Beta-Binomial Model --- conjugacy derivation, update rule
        \item 9 min Think/Pair/Share: \textit{Updating a Beta Prior} worksheet --- apply the update rule to a new dataset and compare priors of different strength (see \texttt{Lecture34\_worksheet.tex})
        \item 4 min Lecture: Example: Back to the Drug Trial --- worked posterior, visualizing the update
        \item 5 min Lecture: The Influence of the Prior --- five oncologists, five posteriors; balancing prior and data as $n$ grows
        \item 2 min Lecture: Sequential Updating, Revisited --- Beta-Binomial specializes the general sequential-update rule from Lecture 33
        \item 2 min Lecture: Credible Intervals --- definition, contrast with Frequentist CI interpretation
        \item 2 min Lecture: Summary
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
